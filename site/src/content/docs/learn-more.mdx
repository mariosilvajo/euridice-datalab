Stuck on something a lesson assumed? These are all **free to read online**.
Keep this page open in a second tab.

## Python basics — variables, lists, loops

- **[Think Python](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/)** — the gentlest start, free and openly licensed
- **[Automate the Boring Stuff](https://automatetheboringstuff.com/)** — practical Python for non-programmers
- **[Official Python tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/)** — the authoritative reference

## Working with data (pandas)

- **[10 minutes to pandas](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/user_guide/10min.html)** — the official quick tour
- **[Python for Data Analysis](https://wesmckinney.com/book/)** — free book by the creator of pandas
- **[Working with missing data](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/user_guide/missing_data.html)** — the official guide

## Charts

- **[From Data to Viz](https://www.data-to-viz.com/)** — choose a chart from your data type
- **[Python Graph Gallery](https://python-graph-gallery.com/)** — copy-paste recipes
- **[Matplotlib tutorials](https://matplotlib.org/stable/tutorials/index.html)** — official guide
- **[missingno](https://github.com/ResidentMario/missingno)** — the missing-data plots used in Track 1

## Machine learning & fairness

- **[scikit-learn user guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/user_guide.html)** — every model, explained
- **[Fairlearn](https://fairlearn.org/)** — open-source toolkit for measuring model fairness

## Policy & ethics

- **[EU AI Act explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/)** — browse the Act by article
- **[Vienna Manifesto on Digital Humanism](https://dighum.ec.tuwien.ac.at/dighum-manifesto/)** — the founding text
- **[European approach to microcredentials](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022H0627%2802%29)** — Council Recommendation 2022/C 243/02
