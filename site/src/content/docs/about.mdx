## EURIDICE

**EURIDICE** — *European Inclusive Education for Digital Society, Social
Innovation and Global Citizenship* — is co-funded by the European Union
under the **Digital Europe Programme** (Grant Agreement No. 101123121). It
delivers education in **Advanced Digital Skills**, contributing to the EU's
Digital Decade ambition of 20 million ICT specialists by 2030, with
particular emphasis on **Artificial Intelligence and Data Science** applied
across disciplines — and a defining commitment to *inclusive* education
that welcomes learners from non-ICT backgrounds.

The consortium spans 25+ universities, research institutes, companies and
cultural organisations across Europe and beyond — including the University
of Iceland, TU Wien, Fraunhofer IESE, Copenhagen Business School, Vrije
Universiteit Amsterdam, Universidade Lusófona… and IIIM.

**[euridice.eu →](https://euridice.eu)**

## IIIM

The **Icelandic Institute for Intelligent Machines** (Vitvélastofnun
Íslands) is a non-profit research institute dedicated to applied research
in artificial intelligence and automation, catalysing innovation through
collaboration between academia and industry. Within EURIDICE, IIIM designs
and delivers **teacher education modules** (Work Package 3) and
**professional training** (Work Package 4) — including this DataLab.

**[iiim.is →](https://www.iiim.is)**

## The DataLab itself

An open, serverless learning environment: Astro Starlight for the site,
JupyterLite/Pyodide for in-browser Python. Read the full technical story on
[How the DataLab works](/how-it-works/). The source is openly available and
any EURIDICE partner can host a mirror.
