## Three learning tracks

1. **Python & Data Foundations** — never coded before? Start here.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis** — load, clean, summarise and visualise real data.
3. **Machine Learning Fundamentals** — train and evaluate your first model, and learn why ethics is part of the job.

*An open learning environment by [IIIM](https://www.iiim.is) for the
[EURIDICE](https://euridice.eu) project — European Inclusive Education for
Digital Society, Social Innovation and Global Citizenship.*
