**The core craft of data science.** Before any model, any dashboard, any
decision — someone has to *understand the data*. That someone is about to
be you.

## What you'll be able to do

- Load tabular data with **pandas** and inspect its shape, types and quality
- Handle missing values defensibly
- Summarise with `describe()`, `value_counts()` and `groupby()`
- Visualise distributions and relationships with **matplotlib**
- Compute and *correctly interpret* correlations
- **Measure how certain a group's number is** before it becomes a headline —
  and handle very small groups responsibly

## The dataset

You'll work with **EU Media Habits 2026** — a survey of 624 respondents in 8
European countries about how they consume news and media: hours of TV,
social media and news per day, their main news source, and how much they
trust the news. *(Synthetic data, generated for teaching — but it behaves
like the real thing, missing values included.)*
