## Checkpoint

You should now be able to answer, with code:

- How many rows and columns does a dataset have?
- Which columns have missing values, and what did you do about them?
- What's the average of a column *per group* (e.g. per country)?
- Is a correlation positive, negative, or meaningless — and why does
  correlation ≠ causation?
- In the ethics lab, only one step of the country ranking was bigger than
  the margin of error. What headline can this data actually support?
- Why is a group of about a dozen people a problem *both* for accuracy and
  for privacy?

**Prove it:** the [graded exercise](/tracks/eda/02-exercise/) asks you six
questions about this exact dataset — with an instant checker.
